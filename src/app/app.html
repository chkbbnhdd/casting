How to use this demo

1. Add videos to the queue from the sample cards or the form. 2. Click the Cast icon and choose your Chromecast or Cast-enabled TV. 3. Click Cast to screen to connect, then Send queue to push the playlist. 4. Use Play selected, Next, Previous, Pause, and Stop to control playback.
